# Lobby artwork

Avatar PNG images a01–a30 are supplied by the project owner in
the composite image codex-clipboard-f1228f99-4f4e-42b7-9923-bc72a5e1f6e6.png.
Portraits are extracted along their outer silhouettes and centered on transparent
512x512 canvases, preserving faces, hair and accessories. The original ZIP is
retained separately in the local review outputs.
The supplied image has no accompanying license or author information. No third-party license
is asserted for these images. File mapping and SHA-256 hashes: avatars/sources.json.
LICENSE-Fluent-Emoji.txt relates to the previous animal set, no longer displayed.

Rock, brick, bouquet and water SVG images are unmodified Twemoji 14.0.2
graphics by Twitter, Inc and other contributors, licensed CC BY 4.0.
Source https://github.com/twitter/twemoji/tree/v14.0.2/assets/svg
License https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Motion and particle arrangement are implemented locally with the Web Animations API.

Current reaction menu artwork and animated projectiles are hand-authored inline SVG/CSS in ui/lobby-social.js and ui/lobby-social.css. The Twemoji item files above are retained legacy assets and are no longer displayed by the current reaction menu or motion.
